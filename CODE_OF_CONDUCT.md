# Community conduct

Treat contributors and people reached by telephone with respect. Do not harass, discriminate, expose private information, or use this project for deceptive, abusive or unsolicited calls. Disclose the AI assistant and respect a recipient's request to stop.

Keep examples and issue reports free of real telephone numbers, account credentials, contact files, recordings and private transcripts. Use synthetic fixtures for tests. Do not post exploit details publicly; use the reporting guidance in SECURITY.md.

Maintainers may remove harmful contributions and restrict participation. For conduct concerns, contact the repository maintainers through GitHub; do not include another person's private information in a public issue.
